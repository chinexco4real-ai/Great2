Dual login auto-switch
Alternates between two tokens. Login 1 runs until it hits its loss limit or loss-streak, then that market's session resets and switches to Login 2. Login 2 runs until it hits its profit target or timer, then switches back to Login 1 — back and forth, non-stop. With multi-market on, every selected market does this independently on its own counters — one market switching never affects any other.

Starts a countdown the moment Login 2 becomes active. Login 2 switches back to Login 1 on whichever happens first: the profit target above, or this countdown running out.
If the profit target hasn't been reached when this runs out, the EA attempts to close any open trade and switches back to Login 1.
Login 1 → Login 2 martingale mode
On: Login 1 always trades normal (flat stake) and Login 2 always trades martingale (win-only doubling, using the max-steps setting above) — per market. You do not need to also turn on the general "Martingale — winning trades only" toggle for this; it's a separate switch and each market runs the login it's currently on independently, no matter what any other selected market is doing. Only applies while dual-login auto-switch is on — has no effect otherwise.

Login 1 → Login 2 reverse mode
Per-market, not global: while this is on, every selected market trades normal direction for as long as it's on Login 1, and automatically flips to reverse direction the moment it switches to Login 2 — independently, based on each market's own current login, not a shared switch. One market can be on Login 1 (normal) while another is already on Login 2 (reverse) at the same time. The main Reverse mode toggle on the right is disabled while this is on, since it isn't needed here — each market's reverse behaviour is driven entirely by its own login state instead.

Pause instead of disable
Login 1 only (dual-login auto-switch off): a simple pause/resume cycle — only the accumulator target profit and pause duration below are active. The instant the combined Login 1 total reaches the target, the result is snapshotted into the log below, every market's stats are reset, and auto-trading is paused for the countdown. Once the countdown elapses, auto-trading resumes automatically from 0 — no login switching involved. With dual-login auto-switch on, this instead turns into a repeating loss→profit cycle that drives the two logins directly. Loss leg: every selected market trades on Login 1; the EA watches the combined Login 1 total against the target loss above, and the instant it's reached, every selected market is switched over to Login 2 (safely — any open trade finishes first). Profit leg: every selected market now trades on Login 2; once the combined Login 2 total reaches the target profit, the result is snapshotted, every market's stats are reset, and auto-trading is paused for the countdown. Cooldown: once the countdown elapses, every selected market switches back to Login 1, auto-trading resumes, and a fresh cycle begins again from the loss leg. Leave this off entirely to keep the original one-shot behaviour — the profit target alone simply switches auto-trading off for good, with no login switching and no auto-resume.

Auto-trade controls
Auto-trading
Places Rise/Fall trades automatically on qualifying signals
Reverse mode
Flips every trade to the opposite direction right before it's placed — a Rise signal executes as Fall (PUT), and a Fall signal executes as Rise (CALL). The signal panel keeps showing the real detected direction; only the trade that's actually sent, logged, and settled is reversed.
Both direction mode
Every signal opens a CALL and a PUT at the same time, each with its own full stake — a Rise signal buys Rise and Fall, a Fall signal buys Fall and Rise. Both legs are logged, tracked, and settled independently. Reverse mode has no effect while this is on, since the same pair of directions fires either way.

Martingale — winning trades only
Off: every trade uses the flat stake above. On: starts at the base stake; each WIN doubles the stake for the next trade (up to the step limit below), then resets back to base. Any LOSS — at any step — immediately resets the next stake back to base. Losing stakes are never increased. Note: if Login 1 → Login 2 martingale mode (in the dual-login section) is turned on, it overrides this toggle per market — Login 1 always flat, Login 2 always martingale — and this switch is ignored while that's active.

The moment that combined total reaches 50, the entire log is cleared. If it's still under 50, it waits another 10 minutes and checks again.
